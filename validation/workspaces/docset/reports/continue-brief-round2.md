## Confirmed

- Lantern Desk is a lightweight internal service desk for mid-sized support teams.
- Current goals are reducing first-response latency for high-priority tickets, giving team leads a single queue health view, and keeping rollout simple enough for self-hosted deployments.
- The product supports ticket intake from email and a basic web form, assignment rules based on team and severity, SLA timers, and a small reporting page with daily queue trends.
- Confirmed constraints and operational notes include 30 days of trend data retention, minimal v1 integrations, the need for clear backup and restore guidance for self-hosted customers, occasional delayed starts for the daily trend job after VM restarts, repeated requests for clearer SLA-breach explanations after reassignment, and customer interest in backup verification guidance during upgrades.

## Suggested Next Messaging

- Emphasize the product as a lightweight, self-hostable service desk focused on faster response times and clearer queue oversight.
- Keep launch and customer-facing messaging concise, avoiding internal implementation details.
- Clearly explain reporting limits, especially the 30-day trend window.
- Add simple customer guidance for backup, restore, and upgrade-time backup verification in self-hosted deployments.
- Improve messaging around how SLA-breached status is determined after ticket reassignment.
